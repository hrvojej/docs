# Research methodology

**As-of:** 2026-09-21

## 1. Research model

Research se vodi u četiri paralelna sloja:

### A. Group / capability discovery
Potpuno mapirati KONČAR Grupu:
- parent i sva izravna društva
- sva neizravno kontrolirana društva
- joint ventures i associates
- novostečena društva
- statusne promjene i pripajanja
- poslovne jedinice koje su pripajanjem prešle u maticu
- lokacije i proizvodni kapaciteti
- R&D, laboratoriji, testiranje i certifikacija
- software/data/AI/cyber/cloud kapaciteti
- embedded/OT/SCADA/PLC/edge kapaciteti
- mehanika, elektrotehnika, power electronics, drives
- manufacturing: machining, welding, tanks, steel, assembly
- field engineering, commissioning i O&M
- postojeći proizvodi, installed base i kupci
- postojeće digitalne platforme i podatkovni izvori
- patenti, EU/R&D projekti i akademska partnerstva

### B. Outside-in market scan
Ne polaziti od postojećih KONČAR proizvoda. Sustavno pregledavati što vodeći industrijski igrači i scale-upovi već prodaju u:
- smart grid / grid automation
- condition monitoring i predictive maintenance
- digital twins
- autonomous inspection
- mobile robotics / drones / ROV / AUV
- industrial computer vision
- AI-assisted engineering
- rail digitalization i autonomous rail operations
- battery / charging / depot optimization
- hydro digital O&M
- wind/solar/BESS O&M
- transformer intelligence
- substation robotics
- asset lifecycle software
- industrial cybersecurity
- edge AI
- industrial data platforms
- remote operations
- additive/advanced manufacturing
- automated welding/inspection/NDT
- data-center energy systems i critical power
- offshore/marine inspection
- defense/dual-use samo gdje je poslovno i regulatorno relevantno

### C. Adjacency mapping
Za svaku opportunity tezu povezati:
**Market need → existing commercial reference → required stack → KONČAR assets → missing gaps → feasible entry mode.**

### D. Validation
Opportunity ne prelazi u shortlist dok nisu provjereni:
- customer pain
- willingness to pay / procurement model
- incumbent solutions
- total cost of ownership
- regulation/certification
- service model
- IP/defensibility
- realistic time-to-market
- gross margin logic
- exportability

## 2. Evidence levels

- **E0 — idea:** neprovjerena hipoteza
- **E1 — technical evidence:** prototip, paper ili demonstrator
- **E2 — commercial evidence:** proizvod postoji i kupci ga koriste
- **E3 — repeatable market:** više dobavljača/kupaca, dokaziva kategorija
- **E4 — KONČAR fit evidence:** identificirani konkretni interni kapaciteti i pilot path
- **E5 — validated opportunity:** customer discovery + economics + implementation path

## 3. Scoring za opportunities

Svaka teza dobiva zasebne ocjene (1–5), bez skrivanja pretpostavki:

1. **Market pull**
2. **Commercial maturity**
3. **KONČAR capability fit**
4. **Cross-company leverage**
5. **Differentiation potential**
6. **Recurring revenue potential**
7. **Export potential**
8. **Time to pilot**
9. **Capital intensity**
10. **Certification / regulatory complexity**
11. **Competitive intensity**
12. **Data advantage**
13. **Installed-base leverage**
14. **Serviceability / lifecycle value**

Ocjene 9–11 se pri agregiranju tretiraju kao penalties.

## 4. Build / Partner / Acquire

Za svaki gap:
- **Build:** postoji dovoljno blizak kompetencijski core
- **Partner:** capability je commodity ili brzo dostupan
- **Acquire:** capability je strateški, teško ga je organski izgraditi i značajno skraćuje time-to-market
