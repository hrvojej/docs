# Global opportunity landscape

**As-of:** 2026-09-21  
**Status:** hypothesis landscape; each family requires commercial evidence pack.

The purpose is to avoid tunnel vision around ROV/AI. Opportunities are grouped by customer problem and global commercial category.

## Opportunity families to research

- substation inspection robots
- overhead-line drone inspection
- transmission-tower inspection
- confined-space robots
- hydro penstock/turbine inspection
- underwater ROV/AUV for dams, intakes, ship/port/industrial assets
- autonomous solar/wind inspection
- robotic cleaning/maintenance
- remote operations centers

**Potential KONČAR leverage:** Dalekovod + HELB + Hydro + Renewables + Institute + Digital + motors/drives.

## Opportunity assessment rule

A global market trend is **not** automatically an opportunity for KONČAR. Each family must be reduced to a specific buyer, job-to-be-done, product boundary and monetization model.

Example:
“AI predictive maintenance” is too broad.

Valid thesis:
“Subscription health-index service for medium-power transformers using online sensor package + fleet analytics + KONČAR service intervention contract.”
