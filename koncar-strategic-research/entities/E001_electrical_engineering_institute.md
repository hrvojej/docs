# E001 — KONČAR – Electrical Engineering Institute

## Verified role
Applied R&D, proprietary high-tech products, diagnostics, testing, calibration, inspection and certification.

## Hard facts
- 65 years of applied R&D.
- 2025 operating income: **EUR 25.14m**.
- **290 employees**; ~80% higher/higher-professional education.
- **9 laboratories** and ~**800 accredited testing/calibration methods**.
- New LAVESP 2 laboratory in Bedekovčina opened July 2026; investment >EUR 20m.
- Monitoring systems have been delivered to **60 countries**.

## Demonstrated capability
- transformer R&D: electromagnetic/electrodynamic/thermal phenomena, insulation, UHV/HVDC, condition assessment;
- rotating machines: advanced design, materials, vibration/noise, diagnostics;
- switching equipment/switchgear research;
- power converters;
- rail vehicle components;
- material/stress/seismic analysis;
- renewable-energy analysis, including hydrogen;
- monitoring/control systems;
- embedded computer systems;
- field diagnostics and NDT;
- certification/conformity assessment.

## Proprietary digital/monitoring IP
### TMS
Transformer Monitoring System with modular sensor integration, component condition models, trend analysis and IEC/IEEE gas-analysis interpretation.

### MCM / EMCM
Rotating-machine condition monitoring. EMCM uses Institute-developed proprietary measurement methods and supports predictive maintenance.

### iPDCore / KonFID
- in-house proprietary hardware platform;
- synchronous high-speed acquisition and real-time processing;
- automatic clustering using modern machine-learning algorithms;
- automatic partial-discharge fault classification;
- applicable to transformers, cables, generators and motors;
- integrated into TMS/MCM and laboratory automation.

## Commercialization assets

- field diagnostics;

- deep domain knowledge across multiple KONČAR equipment families.
