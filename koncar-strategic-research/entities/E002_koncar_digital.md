# E002 — KONČAR – Digital

## Verified role
Digital solutions/software platforms for energy, critical infrastructure and urban infrastructure.

## Demonstrated capability
- >40 years of industrial digital-system development;
- certified solutions deployed at thousands of locations across Europe, Asia, Africa and South America;
- SCADA / real-time control;
- PROZA Station and PROZA HAT platform;
- system integration;
- data-centre monitoring/control (DCIM);
- urban/water infrastructure digitalization;
- grid/energy data integration;
- cybersecurity;
- R&D in IoT, edge/cloud and AI/ML.

## AI / analytics evidence
- **HEDGE-IoT (2024–2027):** edge/cloud intelligence and advanced AI/ML in energy systems.
- **Industrial IoT platform R&D:** predictive maintenance, safety/cybersecurity and energy efficiency.
- **BEYOND Big Data & AI:** predictive/prescriptive analytics, forecasting, predictive maintenance and edge analytics.
- public KONČAR R&D/sustainability material documents software prototypes for **AI integration into SCADA**.
