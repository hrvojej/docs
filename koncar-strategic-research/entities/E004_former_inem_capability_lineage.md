# E004 — Former INEM capability lineage

## Legal status
INEM ceased as a separate subsidiary in 2026:
- Power Supply business unit separated into KONČAR – Helb;
- Assembly business unit incorporated into HELB;
- remaining assets/rights/obligations/employees merged into KONČAR Inc. (court registration 2026-07-31).

This dossier exists because **capabilities survive legal merger**.

## Demonstrated capability before merger
### Power electronics
- converter design using Si IGBT and SiC MOSFET;
- electrical/mechanical/thermal/control architecture;
- traction converters;
- auxiliary converters;
- battery-operation converters;
- frequency converters for renewable/hydro applications.

### Embedded / protection / control
KONPRO 2 R&D developed a next-generation modular microprocessor/embedded platform for:
- protection;
- measurement;
- control;
- distributed HW/SW functions;
- industrial communication integration.

### Rail / traction electronics
Reference: Liepāja tram project:
- main-drive converters;
- auxiliary-drive converters;
- PN12DC battery converter enabling short off-wire operation.

### Small hydro / renewable conversion
SHP Otočac used INEM-developed frequency converters together with KONČAR generation, transformer, switchgear and Digital SCADA technologies.

### EV/charging adjacency
Q1 2026 structure listed **ENAKON MOBILITY** beneath INEM. Current shareholder after restructuring still needs legal verification.

## Strategic significance
This lineage may contain some of the Group’s most relevant competence for:
- motor drives;
- power conversion;
- edge/embedded control;
- robotics actuation;
- battery/charging;
- autonomous mechatronic platforms.

## Critical evidence gap
After 2026 restructuring, we need to map **which engineers, IP, product lines and facilities went to HELB vs KONČAR Inc.** Legal asset-transfer descriptions alone are insufficient for capability planning.
