# E007 — KONČAR – Distribution and Special Transformers (D&ST)

## Ownership/control
- KONČAR voting-right baseline: **67.90%**
- Q1 2026 economic ownership share disclosed separately: **52.73%**

## Verified scale/capability
- regional leader in medium-power, distribution and special transformers;
- products up to **160 MVA / 170 kV**;
- >**10,000 transformers annually**;
- deliveries to >**110 countries**;
- full service: design, manufacturing, transport, installation, field testing, commissioning.

## Product range
- medium-power transformers;
- oil-immersed distribution transformers;
- dry-type distribution transformers;
- special transformers/reactors;
- traction/offshore/mobile-substation and other customer-specific applications.

## Downstream manufacturing
- FEROKOTAO
- NOVI FEROMONT
- Power Engineering Transformatory (PET), Poznań

## Strategic assets
- large installed base;
- recurring service/post-sales organization;
- design/manufacturing/test data;
- global customer base;
- product variants across harsh/special applications.
