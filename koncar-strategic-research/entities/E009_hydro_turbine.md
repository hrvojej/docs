100% KONČAR.

## Verified core
Complete hydro-turbine lifecycle:
- turbine design;
- manufacturing;
- installation;
- refurbishment/upgrades;
- commissioning;
- performance testing;
- diagnostic measurements;
- long-term service/after-sales.

Engineering is custom to site requirements and includes project-management capability.

## Strategic assets
- direct access to hydro plant operators/assets;
- outage/refurbishment workflow;
- diagnostic work;
- mechanical/hydraulic domain;
- lifecycle service.

## Opportunity surfaces — hypotheses

- cavitation/erosion computer vision;
- sonar + vision inspection;
- sediment mapping;
- remote/robotic inspection during reduced outages;
- hydro digital twin;
- condition-based refurbishment planning.
