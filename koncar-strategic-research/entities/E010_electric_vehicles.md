# E010 — KONČAR – Electric Vehicles

## Control
KONČAR voting/control baseline: **85.73%**.

## Verified core
- design/manufacturing/modernization/maintenance of electric rail vehicles;
- low-floor EMUs/DEMUs;
- low-floor trams;
- locomotive modernization;
- long-term fleet maintenance.

## New-development signals
- Bimodal Electro-Diesel Multiple Unit (EDMU);
- Battery Multiple Unit (BMU);
- Battery Electric Multiple Unit (BEMU);
- **Measurement train** for recording track condition, delivered with MER MEC measurement systems.

## Public fleet/reference indicators
Current page lists:
- 353 electric locomotives;
- 194 low-floor trams;
- 70 low-floor trains;
and states >400 locomotives modernized historically.

## Technical/service capability
- workshops/infrastructure for maintenance;
- specialized mechanical/electrical calculations;
- measurement of stress, vibration, displacement, pressure, temperature etc.;
- fleet control/management systems on vehicles.
