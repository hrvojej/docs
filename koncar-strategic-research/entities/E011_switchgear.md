# E011 — KONČAR – Switchgear

## Ownership
100% KONČAR.

## Verified core
Manufacturer of transmission/distribution equipment:
- LV switchgear;
- MV primary/secondary distribution;
- HV equipment;
- GIS;
- transformer substations;
- technical/maintenance services.

Company states it independently develops almost all products in its current portfolio.

## Technical range examples
- 123/145 kV HV circuit breakers up to 4000 A;
- disconnectors/earthing switches up to 420 kV / 4000 A;
- modular GIS;
- MV ON.1/BVK and other distribution products.

## Strategic assets
- proprietary product design;
- substation installed base;
- service access;
- IEC type-testing experience;
- natural interface to Digital SCADA and Institute monitoring.

## Opportunity surfaces — hypotheses
- smart/sensorized switchgear;
- thermal/partial-discharge monitoring;
- edge anomaly detection;
- remote visual/robotic substation inspection;
- condition-based service;
- asset health digital twin.
