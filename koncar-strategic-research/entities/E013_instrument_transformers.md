# E013 — KONČAR – Instrument Transformers

## Control
- 99.77% voting rights reported in Q1 2026;
- 61.97% economic ownership share separately disclosed due share structure.

## Verified core
- HV instrument transformers: 72.5–800 kV;
- MV instrument transformers: 3.6–52 kV;
- special/reference/test transformers;
- precision measurement and HV insulation know-how;
- GREEN LINE environmentally friendlier product line.

## Innovation signal
KONČAR launched a combined power voltage transformer (SVE) described in 2024 as a first-of-kind global product, combining energy transmission and high-accuracy measurement/protection functions.

## Strategic assets
- precise sensing/measurement domain;
- HV insulation;
- global grid applications;
- long asset life (~50 years stated for HV units);
- interface to protection/measurement systems.

## Opportunity surfaces — hypotheses
- digital instrument transformers;
- higher-fidelity edge sensing;
- self-monitoring sensors;
- data products for grid/substation intelligence;
- calibration/health analytics;
- smart-substation packages with Digital + Switchgear.
