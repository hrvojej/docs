# E016 — KONČAR – Transformer Tanks (KTK)

## Ownership
Joint venture controlled by KONČAR:
- **KONČAR 60%**
- **Siemens Energy 40%**

## Verified core
Development/manufacturing/global distribution of custom large-power-transformer tanks.

Applications include:
- offshore;
- low-temperature;
- HVDC;
- phase-shifting and other special transformers.

## Production capability
- tanks >100 t;
- structural steel plus stainless steel/aluminium/copper where needed;
- MAG/MIG/TIG/EPP welding;
- leak testing and NDT: VT/PT/MT/UT;
- trial assembly, blasting, coating and packaging;
- large cranes.

New modern plant in Sesvetski Kraljevec officially opened January 2026.

Public references include Siemens Energy, Royal SMIT, Hitachi Energy, GE Grid Solutions, Kolektor Etra and Hyosung.

## Strategic assets
This is a high-volume/high-quality welding and NDT environment with world-class OEM customer requirements.

## Opportunity surfaces — hypotheses
- robotic welding;
- automated NDT;
- weld-defect CV/AI;
- digital work instructions;
- 3D dimensional QA;
- production digital twin;
- quality traceability.
