# E019 — KONČAR – Power Transformers (KPT)

## Ownership/status
Affiliate / JV:
- **Siemens Energy 51%**
- **KONČAR 49%**

Not controlled as a normal subsidiary, but strategically material to Group capability.

## Verified core
Sale, development, design, manufacturing, testing and servicing of large power transformers:
- generator step-up transformers up to 550 kV / 1000 MVA;
- network/autotransformers up to 550 kV / 1000 MVA;
- HVDC transformers up to 550 kV DC;
- shunt reactors up to 550 kV / 250 MVAr;
- special/rail/furnace/rectifier transformers;
- service, revitalization, diagnostics and maintenance.

## Scale / investment
- ~650 employees reported for 2024.
- KONČAR + Siemens Energy announced ~**EUR 260m** expansion.
- capacity path: ~22,000 MVA → 30,000 MVA by 2027 → **45,000 MVA by 2031**.
- ~350 new jobs expected from expansion.

## Strategic assets
- world-class LPT manufacturing/design;
- high-value installed assets;
- service/diagnostics;
- HVDC/offshore/grid transition exposure.

## Opportunity surfaces — hypotheses
- transformer health/digital twin;
- online condition monitoring;
- lifecycle analytics;
- predictive service;
- manufacturing QA/automation.

## Important constraint
Because Siemens Energy is majority owner, all opportunity work must check:
- IP ownership;
- customer/data rights;
- JV governance;
- overlap with Siemens Energy digital products.

Do not assume KPT technology/data can be freely productized by KONČAR Group.
