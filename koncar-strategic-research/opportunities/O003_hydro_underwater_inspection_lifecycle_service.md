# O003 — KONČAR Hydro Underwater Inspection & Lifecycle Service

## Thesis

Create a hydro-specific **robotic inspection and engineering service** that combines partner ROV/crawler technology with KONČAR's turbine/generator domain expertise and lifecycle service.

The core product is not an ROV.

The product is:

**access underwater → capture reliable evidence → map it to the asset → engineering diagnosis → maintenance/refurbishment decision → KONČAR executes the work**

## Initial customer jobs

High-confidence first use cases:
- intake / trash-rack inspection;
- gate / seal inspection;
- turbine runner/blade visual inspection;
- draft tube / spiral casing;
- dam-face / submerged structure;
- emergency blockage/leak/problem diagnosis;
- pre-refurbishment condition baseline;
- post-repair verification.

Second-stage:
- short/medium penstock visual inspection;
- sonar/3D geometry;
- robotic wall-thickness measurements.

R&D-stage:
- long flooded winding penstock inspection;
- autonomous localization/mapping;
- advanced robotic NDT/EMAT;
- intervention tooling.

## Product ladder

### Tier 1 — ROV Inspection Service
Partner ROV + KONČAR hydro engineer.

Deliverables:
- video/images;
- structured asset-linked findings;
- defect severity;
- engineering conclusion;
- remediation recommendation.

Commercial model:
project/day rate.

### Tier 2 — Hydro Condition Baseline
Add:
- sonar where visibility is poor;
- dimensional references;
- 3D/photogrammetry where useful;
- repeatable inspection positions;
- comparison to drawings/prior inspections.

Commercial model:
higher-value engineering inspection project.

### Tier 3 — Lifecycle Inspection Contract
Periodic inspection + engineering review:
- asset history;
- planned recurring surveys;
- pre/post outage;
- inspection-to-work-order;
- refurbishment planning.

Commercial model:
multi-year service agreement.

### Tier 4 — Advanced Penstock NDT
Only where customer economics prove value:
- crawler;
- UT/corrosion mapping;
- laser/profilometry;
- localized quantitative wall-loss data.

Commercial model:
specialist inspection project / premium service.

### Tier 5 — Proprietary robotic subsystem
Only after repeated missions reveal an unsolved common problem that partner equipment cannot solve economically.

Potential areas:
- tether management;
- hydro-specific navigation;
- standardized turbine scanner;
- NDT payload;
- automated defect mapping.

## Pilot design

### Pilot 1 — low-risk commercial proof
Select a hydro customer/site needing a routine visual inspection.

Target:
- intake/gate/turbine/draft tube;
- no long penetrations;
- partner portable ROV;
- KONČAR Hydro Turbine engineer;
- structured digital report.

Measure:
- diver hours avoided;
- outage/dewatering avoided;
- inspection time;
- quality/actionability;
- customer price/value.

### Pilot 2 — richer digital baseline
Add:
- sonar;
- photogrammetry/3D;
- repeatable image positions;
- defect comparison;
- Digital-hosted asset history.

### Pilot 3 — advanced penstock only if justified
Use crawler/robotic NDT partner on a known penstock integrity problem.

Do not use a technically ambitious 400m flooded penstock as Pilot 1.

## Competitive position hypothesis

KONČAR would compete less with Deep Trekker/VideoRay hardware and more with:
- engineering inspection contractors;
- hydro OEM service organizations;
- diving/NDT contractors.

Potential differentiation:
1. turbine OEM/domain engineering;
2. immediate link to refurbishment/repair;
3. generator + turbine combined lifecycle knowledge;
4. cross-disciplinary Institute diagnostics;
5. digital condition history;
6. vendor-independent robotic hardware.

## Main risk

The basic ROV inspection market may have **low technical moat**.

If the offer is only:
> "we have a small ROV and record video"

local/specialist contractors can compete easily.

Defensibility must come from:
- hydro engineering interpretation;
- integration into outage/refurbishment decisions;
- recurring asset history;
- quantitative measurement;
- service response;
- broader lifecycle contract.

## Strategic R&D option — not initial business

B003 shows an unresolved technical area around:
- long penstocks;
- tether friction;
- navigation/localization;
- quantitative wall condition;
- robotic UT/EMAT.

This could eventually justify proprietary R&D if:
- KONČAR customers repeatedly face the same unsolved problem;
- service value is high;
- external platforms are inadequate;
- cross-market reuse exists.

Until then, treat it as a technology option, not a product commitment.
