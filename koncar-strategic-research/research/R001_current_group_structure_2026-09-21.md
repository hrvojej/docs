# R001 — Current KONČAR Group Structure & Ownership

## Scope

Establish a reliable current legal/organizational baseline for subsequent capability and opportunity research.

This research reconciles:
- current KONČAR corporate pages,
- Q1 2026 consolidated financial statements,
- 2026 investor/status-change notices,
- subsidiary sources where needed.

## Executive summary

Current official KONČAR materials state **16 subsidiaries and one affiliated/joint-venture company with Siemens Energy**, in addition to the parent.

Q1 2026 reporting still contained KONČAR – Electronics and Informatics (INEM) as a direct subsidiary. During summer 2026:
- business units were transferred out of INEM,
- then the remaining INEM entity was merged into KONČAR Inc.

Reconciling those changes produces a current baseline of **16 active direct subsidiaries**.

The main research consequence is that a legal entity map is insufficient. A separate **capability-lineage map** must preserve expertise that moved during mergers/demergers.

## Affiliated / associate company

### KONČAR – Power Transformers (KPT)
- **Siemens Energy: 51%**
- **KONČAR: 49%**
- large power-transformer business;
- strategically material manufacturing and engineering affiliate;
- must be included in capability research even though it is not controlled as a subsidiary.

Do not confuse KPT with **KONČAR – Transformer Tanks (KTK)**, where KONČAR controls 60% and Siemens Energy holds 40%.

## Direct entity removed during 2026

### KONČAR – Electronics and Informatics (INEM)
- Q1 2026: 100%-owned direct subsidiary.
- before final merger, **Power Supply** business unit was separated and transferred to HELB.
- **Assembly** business unit was also incorporated into HELB.
- remaining assets, rights, obligations and employees were transferred to KONČAR Inc. through merger.
- Commercial Court decisions registering merger: 2026-07-31; investor notice published 2026-08-03.

**Research treatment:** INEM remains a first-class capability-lineage node even though it no longer exists as an active legal entity.

## Capability-lineage implications

Track legal entities and capabilities separately.

Examples:
- **INEM** → power-supply + assembly capabilities partly to HELB; remaining capability into KONČAR Inc.
- **KREANCA / Prosperus Growth NEOS** → legal entities merged, Digital/NEOS capability persists.
- **DALEKOVOD MK** → capability absorbed into Metal Structures.
