# R002 — In-house AI, Data & Advanced Analytics Capabilities

## Research question

Does KONČAR already possess meaningful in-house AI/ML/data capability, and where does it sit?

## Answer

**Yes.** Public evidence shows multiple distinct capability nodes, not a single isolated AI team:

1. **KONČAR – Digital** — industrial/energy software, SCADA/OT, IoT, data platforms, edge/cloud analytics and AI/ML R&D.
2. **KONČAR – Electrical Engineering Institute** — domain-specific condition monitoring and ML-based fault analysis embedded into electrical-equipment diagnostics.
3. **NEOS capability lineage inside Digital** — explicit Data Science, ML/AI, Data Engineering, cloud-native and forecasting capabilities.

This matters because the strategic question is not “how does KONČAR acquire AI?” but rather “where can existing AI/data capabilities be productized together with domain hardware, equipment telemetry, installed base and service workflows?”

## Capability node A — KONČAR Digital

### Verified public evidence

#### HEDGE-IoT
KONČAR – Digital participates in the Horizon Europe **HEDGE-IoT** project (2024–2027), involving:
- AI/ML,
- edge/cloud computing,
- distributed energy/grid use cases,
- a Slovenian pilot with grid/energy partners and research institutions.

**Capability signal:** hands-on participation in industrial energy AI/edge architecture, not merely generic corporate IT.

#### Industrial IoT platform R&D
KONČAR public R&D references describe an Industrial IoT platform project covering:
- sensors,
- predictive maintenance,
- cybersecurity,
- energy efficiency.

**Capability signal:** ingestion-to-analytics industrial stack.

#### BEYOND — Big Data & AI
Public project material describes analytics work including:
- predictive and prescriptive analytics,
- predictive maintenance,
- forecasting,
- edge analytics.

#### AI + SCADA
KONČAR sustainability/R&D material documents **software prototypes for integrating AI into SCADA systems**.

**Capability signal:** relevant to future edge-AI / asset-intelligence / autonomous-inspection workflows because it connects AI with operational technology rather than treating AI as a standalone application.

### Additional Digital assets relevant to AI commercialization
- critical-infrastructure / energy software
- SCADA and asset-management adjacency
- data-centre infrastructure
- OT/IT integration
- cybersecurity capability
- PowerCIM / CIM and energy data integration
- energy measurement/data-quality applications

## Capability node B — KONČAR Electrical Engineering Institute

### Scale and R&D infrastructure
Current Institute material reports approximately:
- **290 employees**
- **9 laboratories**
- approximately **800 accredited methods**
- long-standing electrical-engineering R&D/testing role.

### Expert Motor Condition Monitoring (EMCM)
The Institute has a proprietary online condition-monitoring offering for rotating machines.

**Relevant capability:**
- sensor/data acquisition,
- machine condition diagnostics,
- predictive-maintenance workflow,
- electrical-machine domain expertise.

### iPDCore
Institute product/research material states that iPDCore uses **machine-learning algorithms for automatic clustering and classification of partial-discharge faults**.

It is used as a component in transformer/machine monitoring contexts.

**Strategic significance:** this is strong evidence that domain-specific ML already exists inside the industrial R&D organization itself, not only in KONČAR Digital/NEOS.

## Capability topology

The three nodes are complementary rather than duplicative:

| Layer | Strongest visible node | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Electrical-domain diagnostics | Institute | EMCM, iPDCore |
| Sensors / equipment monitoring | Institute + Digital | condition monitoring, IIoT |
| OT / SCADA / grid integration | Digital | SCADA, grid/energy projects |
| Data engineering | NEOS + Digital | pipelines, platforms, energy data |
| Generic ML / Data Science | NEOS | explicit DS/ML/AI practice |
| Edge/cloud AI R&D | Digital | HEDGE-IoT, BEYOND |
| Industrial predictive maintenance | Institute + Digital | monitoring products + IIoT/analytics |
| Cyber + AI | Digital + NEOS | OT cyber + AI Defender |
| Domain equipment data | broader KONČAR Group | transformers, machines, rail, grid, hydro — access/data rights to validate per use case |

## Early strategic inference — NOT YET a validated opportunity

KONČAR appears unusually well positioned to combine:
**physical equipment + domain diagnostics + field/service access + OT systems + data engineering + ML.**

Potential defensibility is more likely to come from:
- proprietary equipment/diagnostic data,
- installed-base access,
- domain-specific models,
- certified integration into critical infrastructure,
- lifecycle service contracts,

than from generic AI models alone.

This is an **inference**, not a market/profit conclusion. It must later be validated opportunity by opportunity.

## What is still unknown

1. Number of dedicated ML/data-science engineers inside Digital vs NEOS.
2. Production MLOps stack and model-governance maturity.
3. Which models are actually deployed in customer production environments.
4. Rights/access to telemetry across KONČAR installed equipment.
5. Reusability of Institute algorithms across equipment families.
6. Internal overlap/organizational boundaries after NEOS acquisition.
7. Whether computer vision expertise exists at meaningful production scale.
8. Whether robotics/autonomy control competence exists as a coherent team.
9. Current AI-related patent portfolio.
10. Revenue attributable to AI/data products vs project engineering.
