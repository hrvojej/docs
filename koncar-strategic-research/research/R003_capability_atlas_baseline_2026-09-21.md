# R003 — Cross-Group Capability Atlas — Baseline

**Verified core:** electric motors, electrical systems and related products.  
**Adjacency to investigate:** high-efficiency drives, sensorized motors, condition monitoring, compact motion systems, robotics drives.

**Verified core:**

- process control,
- automation,

**Verified core:**

**Advanced digital evidence:** condition monitoring and ML-based partial-discharge fault clustering/classification.

### KONČAR – Generators and Motors
**Verified core:** generators, motors and large rotating electrical machinery.  
**Adjacency:** advanced sensing, predictive maintenance, digital twins, service analytics, remote diagnostics.

### KONČAR – Helb
**Verified core:** design, construction, commissioning, management/maintenance of power facilities.  
**2026 capability additions:** Power Supply and Assembly business-unit capability transferred from former INEM lineage.

**Strategic relevance:** strong candidate deployment/service arm for equipment intelligence, inspection, retrofit and lifecycle offerings.

### KONČAR – Metal Structures
**Verified core:** large/complex metal structures and fabrication.  
**Adjacency:** robotic welding, automated NDT, machine vision QA, advanced manufacturing, large robotic/mechatronic structures.

### KONČAR – Switchgear
**Verified core:** switchgear across voltage classes and related equipment.  
**Adjacency:** smart substations, embedded sensing, thermal/visual inspection, remote diagnostics, autonomous substation inspection.

### KONČAR – Renewable Energy Sources
**Verified core:** renewable-energy project development/ownership/operations and related lifecycle activities.  
**Current downstream footprint:** wind and solar SPVs in multiple markets.

**Adjacency:** AI O&M, drone/robot inspection, forecasting, BESS/hybrid optimization, remote operations.

### KONČAR – Electric Vehicles

### KONČAR – Instrument Transformers

### KONČAR – Distribution and Special Transformers

### KONČAR – Digital
**Verified core:**

**Verified core:**

**Ownership:** 49% KONČAR / 51% Siemens Energy.  
**Verified core:** large power transformers, major international manufacturing operation.  
**Adjacency:** high-value transformer diagnostics, digital twin/health index, online monitoring and lifecycle services; commercial/IP boundaries with Siemens Energy must be understood before opportunity assumptions.

## Initial strategic observation

The visible differentiator is the **combination** of:
- industrial equipment,
- engineering IP,
- manufacturing,
- field commissioning/service,
- installed base/customer access,
- labs/diagnostics,
- OT/software/data/AI.

This suggests the most interesting outside-in opportunities are likely those where a digital/AI/autonomy layer increases the value of a physical KONČAR asset or lifecycle service, rather than generic standalone software.

This remains a hypothesis until market benchmarks and customer economics are validated.
