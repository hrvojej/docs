# S001 — Foundation + Group Baseline

## What this session established

The research program is not an “AI/ROV study”. It is an **outside-in strategic technology and new-business program for the full KONČAR Group**.

The research starts from two independently built maps:
1. what KONČAR actually owns/can do;
2. what global industrial markets are already commercializing.

Opportunities are generated only from validated intersections.

## Durable decisions

### Repository / artifact protocol
Every meaningful research output must:
- have an ID,
- be persisted as Markdown,
- be registered in MASTER_INDEX.md,
- state as-of date and status,
- separate fact from inference,
- preserve sources,
- state evidence/confidence,
- list open questions and next validation step.

### Navigation protocol
- MASTER_INDEX.md = complete inventory of research.
- CURRENT_CONTEXT.md = minimum context for a new chat.
- R### = evidence-based research.
- E### = company/capability dossier.
- B### = external market/vendor benchmark.
- O### = specific product/business opportunity.
- S### = durable session capsule.

## Main factual baseline

- Current official structure: parent + 16 subsidiaries + one Siemens Energy affiliate/JV.
- 2026 restructurings make capability lineage separate from legal-entity structure.
- INEM no longer exists as a current legal subsidiary but its capabilities remain in HELB and KONČAR Inc.
- NEOS/Kreanca holding structures were simplified into KONČAR Digital.
- HELB is now 100%-owned.
- KTK and KPT are distinct Siemens Energy relationships.

## Main capability baseline

AI/data is already present in-house:
- Digital: industrial AI/ML/IoT/edge/SCADA research and systems.
- Institute: condition monitoring and ML-based diagnostics.
- NEOS: Data Science, ML/AI, Data Engineering, forecasting and AI R&D.

The Group also combines:
- power equipment,
- rotating machines,
- heavy manufacturing,
- EPC,
- field service,
- rail,
- hydro,
- renewables,
- R&D/testing,
- software/data/AI/cyber.

## Most important early inference

Potential advantage is likely to come from integrated **hardware + installed base + domain diagnostics + field service + software/data/AI**, not from generic AI alone.

This is not yet an opportunity verdict.
