# S002 — First outside-in benchmark: transformer intelligence

## What was researched
Commercial transformer monitoring / asset-intelligence market and its fit with KONČAR.

## Durable result

### Market
The category is mature:
- full-stack OEM ecosystems;
- vendor-neutral retrofit;
- fleet APM;
- remote expert service;
- multi-year SLAs;
- performance/outcome contracts.

### Strong market signal
Siemens Energy announced the 2026 acquisition of Camlin Group, disclosed at ~650 employees and >£90m revenue, specifically to strengthen sensor-based monitoring, analytics and grid intelligence.

### KONČAR surprise
KONČAR already has substantially more native capability than a first-pass assumption suggested.

Institute TMS already provides:
- vendor-independent retrofit;
- sensor integration;
- condition models;
- remote access;
- reporting;
- condition-based maintenance;
- overload and remaining-life support.

Institute sustainability reporting says:
- 780 delivered online monitoring systems;
- 64 countries;
- >60,000 MVA under TMS monitoring.

### Main strategic inference
The gap is probably not “build monitoring technology”.

The gap is more likely:
- fleet-level productization;
- unified software/data layer;
- expert remote service;
- SLA/long-term contract packaging;
- cross-company product ownership;
- recurring-revenue commercialization.
