# S003 — B002 + O002 Autonomous Grid / Substation Inspection

## What was researched
Outside-in commercial landscape for:
- autonomous drone-in-a-box inspection;
- remote/BVLOS grid inspection;
- ground inspection robots;
- drone inspection + asset-data platforms.

## Market result

Commercial maturity is clear:
- Skydio has utility deployments with AEP, SCE and AVECC.
- Percepto has FPL statewide rollout and power-sector remote-inspection references.
- ANYbotics has RTE high-voltage substation work.
- Cyberhawk manages inspection/condition data across >11,500 towers and ~150 SSEN substations.
- Sharper Shape demonstrates LiDAR/IR/RGB/digital-twin inspection models.

The market value is moving from robot hardware toward:
- persistent remote capture;
- repeatability;
- AI/thermal analysis;
- asset history;
- defect/risk workflow;
- work-order integration.

## Key KONČAR finding

Dalekovod-Projekt already publicly states it uses:
- drones;
- optical cameras;
- thermal cameras;
- laser cameras
for maintenance documentation and condition analysis of operating power facilities.

Therefore KONČAR is not at zero in aerial inspection.

## Strategic implication

Do not start by designing a proprietary drone or quadruped.

Potentially defensible KONČAR layer:
- grid defect ontology;
- asset history;
- inspection intelligence;
- Digital/SCADA/EAM integration;
- engineering validation;
- field remediation;
- recurring managed service.

Use partner hardware initially.

## Regulatory result
EU BVLOS generally falls into EASA specific-category operations and requires the appropriate SORA/authorization framework. Regulation is a capability layer, not a post-project formality.

## Opportunity created
**O002 — KONČAR Grid Inspection Intelligence & Remote Inspection Service**

Pilot path:
1. use existing Dalekovod drones to validate inspection-data/workflow product;
2. add one partner docked autonomous drone at a suitable substation;
3. test ground robot only if specific environment economics justify it.
