# S004 — B003 + O003 Hydro Underwater Inspection

## Market result

Two different maturity levels were identified.

### Mature
ROV inspection of:
- intakes;
- gates;
- trash racks;
- turbines/runners;
- draft tubes;
- dam structures
is commercially proven.

Strong examples:
- OPG: six-person dive workflow reduced to two-person ROV crew for runner inspection.
- ESB Ireland: ongoing ROV inspection programme across most hydro sites.
- VideoRay / U.S. Bureau of Reclamation.
- Deep Trekker hydro users and service contractors.

### Advanced / emerging
Harder problems remain:
- long winding penstocks;
- localization;
- tether management;
- quantitative UT/corrosion mapping;
- autonomous inspection.

ENGIE/SHEM's 2023 ROV trial failed its ~400 m target because of pressure/tether constraints; 2026 work is still advancing EMAT-enabled robotics.

## KONČAR implication

KONČAR Hydro Turbine already has:
- hydro-domain engineering;
- diagnostic measurements;
- commissioning;
- maintenance;
- refurbishment;
- worldwide lifecycle support.

Therefore first move should be:
**partner ROV + KONČAR engineering inspection + lifecycle repair path**, not proprietary ROV development.
