# S006 — B005 + O005 Rail Fleet / Battery Intelligence

## Market result

Rail OEMs increasingly attach:
- telemetry;
- predictive maintenance;
- automatic maintenance workflows;
- digital depots;
- battery condition;
- energy management
to multi-year lifecycle contracts.

Examples:
- Alstom HealthHub: 100+ projects, 18k+ cars monitored.
- Hitachi HMAX: 2,000+ trains / 200,000 systems.
- Siemens Railigent X: long-term full-service contracts; 2026 battery-train contract for 61 Mireo Plus B trains includes service up to 30 years.
- ABB: traction battery remote monitoring + performance warranty + SLA.
- Railnova: OEM-neutral telemetry/maintenance platform.
