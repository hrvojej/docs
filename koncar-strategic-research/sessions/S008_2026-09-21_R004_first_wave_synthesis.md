# S008 — R004 First-Wave Cross-Opportunity Synthesis

## Milestone

The first planned outside-in benchmark wave is complete:
- B001 transformers;
- B002 grid inspection;
- B003 hydro underwater;
- B004 heavy fabrication;
- B005 rail/battery;
- B006 renewables/BESS.

Six candidate opportunities exist: O001–O006.

## Main synthesis

A repeated pattern appears across the Group:

KONČAR frequently already owns:
- domain engineering;
- hardware/control;
- customer access;
- field service;
- at least part of the digital capability.

The repeated gap is:
- productization across subsidiaries;
- fleet/portfolio software;
- persistent asset history;
- expert remote workflow;
- recurring lifecycle contract/SLA;
- clear end-to-end product/P&L ownership.

## Robotics finding

For grid and hydro:
- partner for drone/ROV/robot hardware first;
- own the domain inspection intelligence, workflow and service;
- proprietary robotics only after an unsolved repeatable high-value gap is proven.

## Manufacturing finding

O004 is not primarily an external product opportunity.
It is an internal capacity/quality opportunity whose first business case should use factory OEE/bottleneck/contribution-margin data.

## Platform finding

O001/O002/O005/O006 share potential software primitives:
- asset hierarchy;
- telemetry;
- events;
- evidence;
- health;
- work orders;
- remote expert workflow;
- analytics.

Do not build a monolithic common platform yet. Validate domain requirements first.

## Next phase

Shift from breadth to validation:
- internal architecture;
- ownership;
- customer economics;
- pricing;
- pilots;
- unit economics;
- data/IP rights.

R004 is the durable synthesis artifact.
