## Source hierarchy

1. current regulatory filing / audited official filing
2. official corporate status notice
3. official KONČAR/subsidiary site or report
4. patents / EU project / research databases
5. customer/vendor deployment case study
6. reputable trade press
7. registry aggregators / job postings / professional profiles as supporting evidence only

## Research rule

A source proving **legal purpose**, a job ad, or a marketing statement is not automatically proof of operational capability. Artifacts explicitly label such evidence and require corroboration before strategic scoring.
